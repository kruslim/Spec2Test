Widget Lamp Specification

This specification is invented for the Spec2Test extraction tests.

1. SCOPE

1.1 Purpose. This document specifies requirements for a widget lamp.

2. REQUIREMENTS

2.1 Brightness. The lamp shall emit at least 400 lm within 2.0 s of switch-on.

2.2 Housing. The housing shall be red and shall carry a voltage label.

2.3 Switch. The switch shall be operable with one hand.

2.4 Battery. The battery should last 8 h at full brightness.

2.5 Mounting. The lamp shall mount on either:

a. a 35 mm rail; or

b. a wall bracket.
